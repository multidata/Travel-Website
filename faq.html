Frequently Asked Questions

Booking Questions

You can book flights through our website by selecting your destination, dates, and preferred airline. Our booking system will show you the best available options and prices. You can also call our booking hotline at +1-800-TRAVEL for assistance.

Yes, most bookings can be modified up to 24 hours before departure, subject to availability and any applicable change fees. Contact our customer support team for assistance with modifications.

For international flights, we recommend booking 2-3 months in advance for the best prices. For domestic flights, 1-2 months is usually sufficient. Peak travel seasons may require earlier booking.

Payment Questions

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), debit cards, and PayPal. We also offer installment payment plans for certain packages.

Our cancellation policy varies by booking type. Most bookings can be cancelled up to 24 hours before departure for a full refund, minus any processing fees. Some non-refundable tickets may have different terms.

Refunds are typically processed within 7-10 business days. The time may vary depending on your bank or credit card provider. You'll receive an email confirmation once the refund is processed.

Travel Questions

For international travel, you'll need a valid passport and may require visas depending on your destination. We recommend checking with the embassy of your destination country for specific requirements.

Yes, we offer comprehensive travel insurance packages that cover medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost luggage, and more. You can add insurance during the booking process.

If your flight is delayed or cancelled, contact our 24/7 support team immediately. We'll help you rebook or find alternative arrangements. Keep all receipts for any additional expenses.

Support Questions

You can reach our customer support team via phone at +1-800-TRAVEL, email at support@travelwebsite.com, or through our live chat feature available on all pages.

Our customer support is available 24/7 for urgent matters. Our main office hours are Monday-Friday 9AM-6PM EST, and Saturday 10AM-4PM EST.

Yes, we offer support in multiple languages including Spanish, French, German, and Mandarin. Please let us know your preferred language when contacting us.
